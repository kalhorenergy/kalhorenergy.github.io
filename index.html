About KALHOR ENERGY

KALHOR ENERGY is an independent blockchain initiative focused on building a transparent, secure and decentralized Proof-of-Work network. The project aims to begin with a simple and independently mineable blockchain, prioritizing security, verifiability and open-source development.

Current project status:
KALHOR ENERGY is in its early development stage. Work completed so far is limited to a local prototype. There is currently no public mainnet, operational public mining network, or publicly transferable KALH asset.

Technology & Principles

Proof of Work

A planned consensus model inspired by Bitcoin, designed around computational work and independent verification.

Decentralization

The long-term objective is an open network that does not depend on a single central operator.

Open Source

Development is intended to be transparent, publicly reviewable and accessible to contributors.

Future Ecosystem

Smart contracts and decentralized applications may be considered only after the base blockchain is developed and independently tested.

Development Roadmap

Phase 1 — Foundation

Project architecture, principles and initial local prototype.

Phase 2 — Core Blockchain

Development and security testing of the core Proof-of-Work chain.

Phase 3 — Public Testnet

A public testing environment, subject to successful internal development and review.

Phase 4 — Mainnet Preparation

Independent review, security assessment and launch readiness evaluation.

Phase 5 — Future Ecosystem

Potential smart contracts and decentralized applications after the base network is secure and stable.
