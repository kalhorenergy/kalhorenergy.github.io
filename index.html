About KALHOR ENERGY

KALHOR ENERGY is an independent blockchain project in early development. Its vision is to build a decentralized, open-source digital network based on transparent rules, public participation and verifiable technology.

The project draws inspiration from Bitcoin's core principles: Proof of Work, decentralization, network security and a transparently limited supply. Its long-term vision includes exploring smart contracts and decentralized applications after the foundational blockchain has been developed and tested.

Technology & Vision

Proof of Work

A mining-based consensus model designed around computational work and verifiable block production.

Decentralization

A long-term commitment to open participation, transparent rules and reduced dependence on central control.

Open Source

A development vision built around public documentation, reviewable code and community participation.

Future Expansion

Smart contracts and decentralized applications may be explored after the base network reaches sufficient maturity.

Development Roadmap

  1. Phase 1 — Foundation: Initial concept, project structure and local prototype.
  2. Phase 2 — Validation: Strengthen blockchain logic, supply rules and security testing.
  3. Phase 3 — Public Development: Publish technical documentation and invite independent review.
  4. Phase 4 — Test Network: Explore a public testnet after the core implementation is ready.
  5. Phase 5 — Mainnet Research: Evaluate security, decentralization and launch readiness.
  6. Phase 6 — Future Ecosystem: Explore smart contracts and decentralized applications.

Our Principle

Build carefully. Verify openly. Develop transparently. Long-term trust must be earned through technology, security and accountability.
